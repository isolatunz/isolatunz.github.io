Mental Health

🌿 What Is Stress?

Stress is a normal reaction to heavy, overwhelming and challenging life situations or pressure. Stress may cause your body or mind to feel tense but stress is also your brain's way of protecting you. This means that stress is not always a bad thing. Sometimes stress can help you remember about a deadline or react quickly when you need to. Stress becomes a problem when it isn't just a temporary feeling anymore, it starts lasting for weeks and affects your daily life.

What does Stress feel like?

Stress can feel different for everyone. Sometimes it can be a feeling of a busy or worried mind but stress can be felt physically. It can cause your body to be tense, make it harder to relax or affect your energy, daily routine and show up in ways you dont realize.

Common signs include:

  • Fast heart rate
  • Having a busy mind or constantly overthinking
  • Feeling tired even after resting
  • Having trouble focusing or staying organized
  • Feeling tense, restless, or easily irritated
