About Us

NextStepNYC was created to help teens find opportunities, resources and wellness information. In this website we will share information about opportunities available around NYC and share wellness tips to support students. Our goal is to make it easier for teens to learn about mental health, wellness and gain opportunities that'll prepare them for their future and support them during the process. Whether you’re looking for opportunities, ways to improve your health, or to learn ways to help yourself and those around you, NextStepNYC is here to help.

Why I Started It

As a high school student, I know it can be hard to find opportunities and resources without having support or connections. Therefore, I created NextStepNYC to make this process easier and to share information to help students succeed and know they’re not alone.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to help students find the resources, programs and opportunities they need to grow, learn, and succeed. NextStepNYC will continue adding new resources, opportunities, wellness articles, and programs for NYC teens. Our goal is to help students discover possibilities, build confidence and prepare for their future.
